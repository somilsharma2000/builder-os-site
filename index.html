Straight answers

"Humara sab WhatsApp pe hota hai."

Perfect — Builder OS lives on WhatsApp. Your staff don't learn a new portal; leads, reminders and confirmations flow where your team already works. The system just makes sure nothing gets forgotten.

"Excel kaam karta hai humara."

Excel shows what you typed. Builder OS shows what you forgot — the follow-up that never happened, the installment due last week, the broker's lead that became someone else's booking.

"RERA wale kaam mein kya karega?"

Your CA gets a structured quarterly export in one click instead of asking your staff to rebuild data from screenshots. We are a data system, not a compliance advisor — your CA stays fully in charge of filings.

None. The app is used by you or one office person for five minutes a day. Everything else — reminders, records, calculations — happens automatically. That's the point of a managed system.
